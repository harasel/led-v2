The Full Picture

LED Is Only Half the Story.

Swapping old fittings for LED is a great start — but it's only part of the opportunity. Real commercial lighting savings come from combining efficient fittings with smarter control over how, when and how bright your lights operate.

01

LED Efficiency

Modern LED fittings deliver significantly more light per watt than legacy fluorescent or halogen systems — immediately reducing energy consumption across your facility.

02

Occupancy Sensing

Automatically reduce or switch off lighting in vacant areas like corridors, stairwells and common areas — so you only illuminate spaces when someone is actually there.

03

Intelligent Dimming

Reduce light output when full brightness isn't needed — such as during daylight hours or in areas where occupancy has been detected but full output is unnecessary.

04

Scheduling & Timing

Program lighting schedules that match your actual building usage — reducing unnecessary operating hours during nights, weekends and holiday periods.

The Challenge

Common-area lighting across five buildings was operating continuously — regardless of whether spaces were occupied. The facility needed a smarter way to reduce unnecessary lighting operating time while maintaining appropriate illumination for residents and staff.

The Solution

LED Solutions designed and installed a complete LED lighting upgrade combined with occupancy sensing, intelligent dimming and timing controls — programmed to match the actual usage patterns of each building and common area.

The Result

Approximately 43% of lighting across the facility can now be switched off when areas are vacant, while the remaining lighting operates at around 30% output when dimmed — maintaining appropriate light levels while significantly reducing unnecessary energy consumption.
